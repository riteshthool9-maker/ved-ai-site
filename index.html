The Product

More than a chatbot. A persistent execution layer.

Most AI experiences stop at generating an answer. Ved is being built to maintain continuity across work, coordinate tools and knowledge, verify what happened and recover when execution fails.

01

Memory & continuity

Maintains useful context across conversations and longer-running work.

02

Planning & execution

Breaks goals into actionable steps and coordinates tools to carry them out.

03

Knowledge intelligence

Uses retrieval, specialist routing and structured reasoning to work across different domains.

04

Verify, recover, continue

Checks task outcomes, detects failures and can recover or replan rather than silently stopping.

Architecture

A complete agent loop, not disconnected AI features.

Ved combines model intelligence, memory, retrieval, tools, verification and recovery inside one coordinated runtime.

Understand → Plan → Act → Observe → Verify → Recover → Answer
